Methodology

How we work

The discipline is the differentiator. Every choice below is codified in the pipeline, not aspirational.

Bayesian MrP, properly executed
Four-chain NUTS sampling with convergence diagnostics on every fit. ACS poststratification to the target geography. Every estimate ships with a 95% credible interval — point estimates without uncertainty are not delivered.
Honest minimums
No subgroup claim ships below N=100. The Pew minimum is the floor regardless of who is reading. Unreliable data is unreliable, and we say so rather than smooth it over.
Correlation language
Cross-sectional surveys support association, not causation. We do not write “moves,” “drives,” or “causes” about message-pair data — the rule is enforced in the renderer, not just the style guide.
Standing data-integrity invariants
Every silent-data bug we have ever caught is now a permanent automated check. The pipeline cannot ship results that violate any prior invariant — pagination, provenance, vocabulary, minimum-N, ESS gates — and the check runs on every fit.
Provenance on every number
Every percentage on every deliverable declares whether it is MrP-adjusted or raw. The labeling is checked automatically. When sources mix on the same surface, each value is labeled distinctly so a reader cannot conflate them.
Honest absence
When the data does not support a claim, the deliverable says so. Empty cells with explanation are preferable to filled cells with false confidence. Validator-rejected output is fixed at the producer, not graceful-degraded to boilerplate.
Two-voice review
Every methodology decision passes a quantitative review (does the statistics hold?) and a practical-engineering review (does the pipeline implement it correctly?) before it reaches a client surface. No solo calls on data discipline.
