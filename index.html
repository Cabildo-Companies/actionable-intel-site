Capabilities

From fielded survey to targeted ad buy

One integrated stack — survey intelligence, voter modeling, and media activation.

📊

Bayesian MrP modeling

Multi-level regression with poststratification anchors raw survey responses to district-level ACS population targets. Every estimate ships with 95% credible intervals.

🗳️

Voter Intelligence Profiles

VIP scores classify respondents into persuasion archetypes — Committed, Reform-Ready, Pragmatists, Skeptics — grounded in behavioral indicators, not self-reported identity.

🎯

Media activation

VIP segment data converts directly into platform-native targeting parameters for Meta, X Ads, and StackAdapt — no voter file required for the issue-advocacy path.

✍️

MediaMaker

AI script generation grounded in your survey data. Every output cites the numbers from the field — support percentages, persuasion gaps, segment findings — never invented statistics.

Process

Field to decision in days, not weeks

The pipeline runs automatically. Partners see results in the portal as soon as the fit completes.

Survey fields

Issue-advocacy questions deployed via Alchemer to a representative sample in the target state or district.

Engine fits

The VIP engine runs 4-chain NUTS sampling, raking to ACS margins, and per-segment MrP — producing MrP-adjusted support estimates for every question.

Portal delivers

Partners log into the ActInt portal to review topline MrP results, regression findings, and audience breakdowns with credible intervals.

Campaign activates

VIP segments export as targeting briefs. MediaMaker turns survey evidence into draft scripts. Media buyer executes directly from the data.
